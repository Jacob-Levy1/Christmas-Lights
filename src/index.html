Paint with your finger. Front maps your drawing onto the half of the tree facing you; Wrap wraps it all the way round.
300 pixels is a very small screen — bold, high-contrast pictures read best. The front projection corrects for the cone, so the picture looks right when you stand in front of the tree.
These run on the tree itself, so they keep going after you close this page.

Sign in

The tree's setup is behind a password so a visitor cannot reshape it. Anyone can still play the games and change the idle pattern without signing in.
Over the tree's own hotspot this is plain HTTP, so treat the wifi password as the real lock. Signing in keeps guests out of the settings; it is not protection against someone already on the network who is watching the traffic.

Tree shape

Loop 1
Tap a bar to pick a loop, then move pixels between it and the next one up until the lengths match your tree. “Auto” goes back to working them out from the cone shape.

Raw setup

Type the tree out instead of nudging sliders. Level 0 is the bottom. Counts can add up to less than the strip — whatever is left over at the end stays dark.
Accepted ways of writing it 
0 20l, 1 17l, 2 15l   level then count
0: 20                 one per line
1: 17
20, 17, 15            counts only, bottom first
20 17 15              spaces work too
lead-in 12            pixels before loop 1
# a hash starts a comment

Centre each loop on the front

One pixel lights up. Slide until it is pointing straight at the front of the tree, then save it. Do a few loops and let the rest be filled in, or do them all.

Check it

“Front line” lights one pixel per loop at the front. When the calibration is right they form a straight vertical line up the front of the tree.

Hardware

Two 300-LED reels chained together are 600 here. They must be joined end to end on the one data pin; a second reel on its own pin is not supported.

Optional. Wire each switch between its pin and ground — no resistors needed. While the tree is running its own show the buttons change pattern (◀ ▶) and brightness (▲ ▼), and select starts Snake on the tree itself. While a phone is playing, the buttons work as a controller for it.
